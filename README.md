## 👋 About

I am a **Technical Founder and Systems Architect** with an academic background in **Physics and Astrophysics**. I work at the intersection of enterprise software, AI systems, behavioural intelligence, data infrastructure and product architecture.

My approach to engineering comes from treating software less like a collection of features and more like a dynamic system: components have boundaries, state moves through the system, constraints matter, feedback changes behaviour, and architecture should remain intelligible as complexity grows.

I am currently building **Polynovea**, where this thinking is being applied across enterprise operating systems and behavioural-intelligence research.

## 🚀 Current Focus

- **Infrakinetic** — a Polynovea enterprise operations platform built around modular business engines, shared platform services, governance and multi-tenant architecture.
- **Human Behavioural Intelligence Framework (HBIF)** — research into mathematical and computational structure across behaviour, decisions, actions, outcomes, evidence and learning.
- **Research & Data Infrastructure** — acquisition pipelines, asynchronous processing, model-support systems and tooling for large, noisy or fragmented datasets.
- **Platform Architecture** — event-driven systems, tenant isolation, schema-driven configuration, observability, governance and automation.

## 💡 Engineering Principles

- **Systems over isolated features** — reason about state, boundaries, flows and failure modes before adding surface area.
- **Configuration over forks** — prefer declarative schemas and controlled extension points to customer-specific code branches.
- **Strong boundaries** — separate engines, shared platform services, governance and data ownership deliberately.
- **Event-driven where it earns its complexity** — use asynchronous systems when decoupling, throughput or recovery genuinely benefits.
- **AI as an augmentation layer** — integrate models where they improve a workflow rather than forcing AI into the centre of every system.
- **Evidence over assumption** — instrument, test, measure and change systems based on observed behaviour.

## 🎓 From Astrophysics to Systems Engineering

<details>

My systems perspective began in the physical sciences. At the **University of Mumbai (BSc Physics)** I built a foundation in mathematical reasoning, modelling and computational problem-solving. At the **University of Glasgow (MSc Astrophysics)** that expanded into statistical modelling, numerical methods, scientific computing and high-dimensional data analysis.

I later moved into industry across **Accenture**, **Santander UK** and **Atomberg Technologies**, working around data, operations and business systems. Those environments added a second layer to the scientific training: software is not useful because the architecture diagram is elegant; it is useful when it maps correctly onto real operational constraints, incentives, workflows and decisions.

Founding **Polynovea** brought those threads together — physics-inspired systems thinking, enterprise operations, software architecture, AI engineering and behavioural research.
